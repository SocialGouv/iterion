{{- if and .Values.networkPolicy.enabled (not .Values.networkPolicy.ingress.controllerNamespaces) (not .Values.networkPolicy.ingress.extraAllow) }}

!! WARNING (#2804): networkPolicy.enabled=true with BOTH
   networkPolicy.ingress.controllerNamespaces and
   networkPolicy.ingress.extraAllow empty: the server port's rule renders
   with NO from: selector and admits EVERY in-cluster pod — any of them
   can forge X-Forwarded-For (the audit IP, the rate-limit key). The
   render is kept, never refused, for upgrade compatibility; the policy
   carries the iterion.io/ingress-admit-all annotation. Set
   networkPolicy.ingress.controllerNamespaces to your controller's
   namespaces.
