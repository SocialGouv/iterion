# ADR-161 — One resolver decides which credential a call spends; dispatch, admission and meter read its answer verbatim

- **Status**: Proposed — the operator chose the structural change on 2026-11-04 (debt-leap question #107, option A: one resolver shared by the three readers, a short ADR, then a new lane, with the #2022 branch as the witness bench). Design only: nothing below is built. Decisions 5 and 6 and the ordering at the end of 9 wait for the operator; 1–4, 7 and 8 are proposed for review with the lane's first unit.
- **Date**: 2026-11-04
- **Deciders**: jo (direction, #107), Claude (analysis, design)
- **Extends**: [ADR-131](131-tenant-gateway-credential.md) §7 (the stamp's typed refusal is decided once, by the resolver, and reaches the delegate backends per decision 5)
- **Relates to**: [ADR-085](085-pi-as-execution-backend.md) (pi's credential order becomes an arm) · [ADR-132](132-a-run-reads-no-environment.md) (the pod posture is a process-level read, snapshotted per attempt) · [ADR-155](155-reserved-capacity-is-a-durable-allocation.md) (keys on the fingerprint this record's `Spend` carries)
- **Serves**: [#2022](https://github.com/SocialGouv/iterion/issues/2022); witness bench `leap/usagecap-ambient-key` at `6e2389644`

## Context

Three readers answer "which credential does this call spend?", each with a derivation of its own (sites on `6e2389644`):

| Reader | Sites |
|---|---|
| **Dispatch** | claw: `Registry.ResolveWithContext` in process; `forwardableProviderEnv` / `applyForfaitAcrossSandbox` for the sandbox, after which the in-container runner walks the crossed env again; `secrets.GuardSubscriptionOAuth` at the head of `ClawBackend.Execute`. claude_code: `selectedAnthropicCredEnvForCLI`, `ambientStampRefusal`. pi: `piResolveEnv`, `piRouteEnv`, `piSandboxEnv`. codex: `codexCredEnvForCLI`. |
| **Admission** | `runtime.admitSandboxCredentials` (#1768) over `model.ClawSandboxCredential`, `delegate.ClaudeCodeSandboxCredential`, `delegate.PiSandboxCredential` — each recomposes "the env the dispatch composes" and parses the hint chain itself. |
| **Meter** | pre-flight: `runCredKeys.routeKey`, `supervisorKey`, `clawUnslottedKey`, `defaultKey` (`pkg/runner/usage_cap.go`) over `model.AmbientEnvMeter` and `delegate.PiAnthropicRouteSource`; ledger: `credentialSlotForRoute` (`loop_cred_spend.go`) over the `metricsEmitter` routes — only claude_code stamps what it spent (`delegate_finished.fingerprint`), every other backend is re-derived from (backend, model) without the hint. |

Measured on #2022: fifteen commits on the bench; from the second on each answers a review round, and each round found the question answered a new way (fleet records t49, t50):

- **meter ≠ dispatch** — a stamped sandboxed claw walk "meters nothing" while the crossing forwarded the pod's `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` and the container spent it (t49 r1, MEDIUM); the ledger booked a claw `anthropic/claude-*` route on a z.ai key no claw path spends (t49 r2, MEDIUM); under `ITERION_FORBID_SUBSCRIPTION_OAUTH` the pre-flight parks a run on the pod's account for a node `GuardSubscriptionOAuth` refuses before either arm (t50 r2, MEDIUM, **open**).
- **crossing ≠ in-process dispatch** — fixing the crossing refused a node its run's forfait funds once a z.ai key sat beside it (t49 r2, MEDIUM, a regression); the crossing judged the forfait on the raw `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL` and ignored `provider: "anthropic"` (t50 r1, MEDIUM).
- **meter ≠ meter** — the route meter and the supervisor meter disagreed on one spec behind a redirected wire or under the switch (t50 r1, MEDIUM).
- **admission ≠ dispatch** — the probe splits the hint on its first comma, the dispatch parses the chain (`resolveProviderChain`: env expansion, `provider:model` elements): documented spellings pass at dispatch and are refused at launch (t50 r2, LOW, **open**).
- **one backend ≠ the others** — pi and codex read no stamp: a stamped run's pi node spends the pod's key and the meter names that account (t49 r2, MEDIUM, deferred).

Every fix was right where it landed; the next round found the reader it missed. That is the Nth-variant test failing (AGENTS.md, philosophy 2): each new posture — stamp, switch, redirected wire, hint, a key beside a forfait — is an edit in N readers. More readers carry the same question: `anthropicFunding` (`executor_funding.go`) and `ctxFundsProvider` (`pkg/supervise/bot.go`), both hint-blind (t50 r1, deferred), `forfait.Check` (reads `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` to decide whether the forfait is in use), and the auxiliaries ADR-131 §7 lists.

The stamp (`RunBundle.AmbientLLMRefused`) has no producer on main; #2005 PR-C' writes it. What the stamp binds is free to decide today and a launch-breaking change after PR-C'.

## Decision

1. **One resolver.** `pkg/backend/credroute` holds the types, the posture snapshot, the refusal and a registry of per-backend **arms**: `credroute.Resolve(ctx, Call) (Spend, error)`. An arm is its backend's precedence moved out of the dispatch verbatim — claw's key order, `anthropicEnvWalk` and `resolveAnthropicDirect`; claude_code's `AnthropicWireSlotOrder`; pi's store, bearer, OAuth token, key; codex's key, forfait — and registers from its own package, as `RegisterForfaitFirst` does. A backend with no arm is refused at launch by name, never by an engine `if`.
2. **Inputs, all explicit.** `Call`: backend, spec, the hint of ONE leg, role (node, supervisor, auxiliary) and sandboxed. Legs come from one parser, the dispatch's (`resolveProviderChain`: env expansion, `ir.SplitProviderStep`, the launch override), exported as `credroute.Legs` for the probe and the meter. Run: `secrets.Credentials` — keys, pinned keys, forfaits, fingerprints, gateway pairs, `AmbientRefused`. **Posture**: the pod env the resolver may read — the Anthropic channels and the `ANTHROPIC_CUSTOM_HEADERS` credential lines, `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL` classified (direct, z.ai, gateway), `ZAI_API_KEY`, `ITERION_FORBID_SUBSCRIPTION_OAUTH`, the `OPENAI_COMPATIBLE_*` pair, the host logins, the pi and codex stores — snapshotted once per attempt and carried on the context, so the three readers judge one posture.
3. **Output.** `Spend{Channel, Slot, Fingerprint, BaseURL, Source, Metered, MeterKey}` — Channel ∈ run key, pinned key, run forfait, gateway pair, pod env, backend's own config; `Source` in the label grammar `slotOfSource` already parses — or a typed refusal (7). The dispatch builds the client, the spawn env and the crossing **from** the Spend and never walks again; the crossing hands the container the Spend's channel and an explicit empty under every other credential-shaped name, so the in-container runner builds from what it is handed or refuses.
4. **The readers consume it verbatim.** Dispatch: builds from the Spend and stamps `Spend.Source` on the call's usage events (8). Admission: one `Resolve` per node's first leg and per supervisor; the three `*SandboxCredential` probes become that call, the host-state mount and the host codex forfait copy being channels of their arm. Meter: the pre-flight keys `Spend.MeterKey` — `routeKey`, `supervisorKey`, `clawUnslottedKey` and `defaultKey` fold into it; the ledger books the stamped source, and a route that carries none books on nobody under the existing warning, never re-derived. The publisher's walk (`attributedRoutes`, `fundedProviderSet`) stays provider-granular: it predicts what to seal and never sees the pod; it shares the leg parser and the route vocabulary, nothing else.
5. **(a) The stamp binds every backend — operator to decide.** (i) *Recommended*: the stamp is a property of the run — the bundle field's own contract reads "this run's LLM routes are funded by the credentials of this bundle ONLY" — so the pi and codex arms refuse the pod's money under it, and the own-config backends (kimi, grok, opencode) answer the refusal on a stamped run: iterion cannot show they spend nothing of the pod. (ii) claude_code and claw only, ADR-131 §7's letter: pi and codex keep spending the pod on a stamped run and the meter names that account — the bench's state. (iii) A per-backend opt-in. Cost of (i): a stamped run with such a node and no run credential for it is refused at launch; no run is stamped before PR-C', so nothing breaks today. The escape hatch stays where the stamp is decided, the publisher's walk: an unstamped run keeps the pod's money.
6. **(b) pi's pod money — operator to decide.** Under the stamp: (i) *Recommended*: everything not sealed in the run — the inherited `piAnthropicEnvChannels`, every name `piCredentialEnvNames` forwards into the sandbox, and pi's own store (host `auth.json`, `ITERION_PI_AGENT_DIR` / `PI_CODING_AGENT_DIR`); the stamped spawn gets explicit empties under every forwarded name, the run's bound key (`piBindKey`) and an empty per-attempt agent dir. (ii) The same, but an operator-pinned `ITERION_PI_AGENT_DIR` may serve a stamped run — that store is the operator's credential set, the pod's money by definition. (iii) The Anthropic channels only — the other forwarded provider keys (thirty-odd names) stay spendable on a stamped run.
7. **(c) Refusal scope and type — proposed.** Per call: one leg of one node or auxiliary. A refused leg lets the chain's next leg resolve; a node is refused when no leg resolves; admission refuses the launch on the first such node and names it. One type, `*credroute.Refusal{Backend, Provider, Spec, Leg, Reason}`, Reason ∈ {ambient-stamp, subscription-switch, hint-unfunded, no-credential, no-arm}, unwrapping to today's sentinel for its reason (`model.ErrAmbientCredentialRefused`, `secrets.ErrSubscriptionOAuthForbidden`, `model.ErrProviderHintUnfunded`, `delegate.ErrNoFacadeCredential`) so the retry classifier, the run view and every `errors.As` keep reading it. Admission maps the Reason to its failure code: `SANDBOX_CREDENTIAL_UNREACHABLE` for no-credential only; ambient-stamp and subscription-switch get codes and remedies of their own (t50 r2: "export the key the backend forwards" is no remedy for a stamped run). The meter reads any refusal as not metered. `GuardSubscriptionOAuth` becomes a refusal of the claw and pi arms instead of a pre-check of `Execute` — t50 r2's open MEDIUM, closed by construction.
8. **(d) The ledger learns the spend, not the hint — proposed.** (i) The implementer's deferred option: claw's usage events (`llm_request`, `llm_step_finished`, the sandbox relay, the `metricsEmitter` route key) carry the hint, and the ledger re-derives — one more derivation kept alive. (ii) *Recommended*: every backend's usage events carry `credential_source` = `Spend.Source`, written by the process that built the call — the executor in process; in the container the runner, from the IOTask that already carries the hint and now carries the source beside it; claude_code's `fingerprint` is that value under its existing name. Additive event data: an event without it books on nobody under the existing warning.
9. **Migration — dispatch first, claw first.** A resolver written beside the readers is one more derivation, so it is extracted from the dispatch. The lane starts from `6e2389644`: its fixes are the reference behaviour the witnesses pin, and a start from main reopens what they closed.
   - *U0 — the parity table.* Rows (posture × run credentials × backend × spec × hint × sandboxed × stamped) drawn from the bench, each asserting one triangle: the credential the built client or the crossed env carries = the admission verdict = the meter key. Seen red on the bench at the two open t50 r2 rows before anything moves.
   - *U1 — claw.* The registry walk, the crossing, `applyForfaitAcrossSandbox` and the switch guard move into the claw arm; `ClawSandboxCredential` becomes `Resolve`. `claw_sandbox_stamp_test.go`, `ambient_env_meter_test.go` and `sandbox_credential_admission_test.go` keep their assertions; only their call sites move.
   - *U2 — the meter.* The pre-flight on `MeterKey`, `credential_source` on claw's events, `credentialSlotForRoute`'s claw arm deleted; `usage_cap_ambient_meter_test.go` keeps its assertions. #2022 closes here.
   - *U3 — claude_code* (`AnthropicRouteSource` already answers both of its sides). *U4 — pi and codex*, under 5 and 6: the only rows whose expected values change, each naming its decision; `credentialSlotForRoute` goes. *U5 — the auxiliaries*: `anthropicFunding`, `ctxFundsProvider`, `forfait.Check`, the supervisors and ADR-131 §7's list.
   - *U6 — the choke point.* A source-scan guard refuses a read of a credential-shaped name (`envtrust.CredentialShapedName`) on the LLM path outside the posture snapshot.
   - **Ordering with #2005 — operator to decide.** Recommended: PR-C', the stamp's producer, lands after U4, so the first stamped run meets one answer on every backend.

## Consequences

- A new posture or credential kind is one arm edit and its parity rows; a review checks one function, not N readers.
- The meter cannot name an account the call did not spend, and admission cannot admit a node the dispatch refuses: both read the dispatch's own answer.
- The container stops resolving: it receives one channel. Behaviour changes only where the bench already closed a gap (a container-local value filling an absent name).
- Usage events gain `credential_source`, additive; the ledger's re-derivation is deleted, not kept as a fallback.
- `leap/usagecap-ambient-key` does not land as it stands: its code is the starting point, its witnesses the parity spec.
- Out of scope: the publisher's provider walk, the credential pool and reserved capacity (ADR-155) — they key on `Spend.Fingerprint`, unchanged.
