## Context

Four readers answer "which credential does this call spend?", each its own way (sites on main; † only on the bench):

- **Dispatch** — claw `Registry.ResolveWithContext` and the crossing (`forwardableProviderEnv`), re-walked in the container; claude_code `selectedAnthropicCredEnvForCLI`; pi `piResolveEnv` and `piCodexSeed`; codex `codexCredEnvForCLI` — each over the chain `resolveProviderChain` builds (overrides, vars, the allowlist's `dropForbidden`).
- **Admission** — `admitSandboxCredentials` (#1768: fork lane, sandboxed starts only), three probes recomposing the dispatch's env from the raw fields.
- **Meter** — the pre-flight's `AnthropicWireRoutes` (a third chain parser, first leg only), `routeKey`, `supervisorKey`, `clawUnslottedKey`†, `AmbientEnvMeter`†, and the supervisor filters `PoolSurvivingSpecs` and `AmbientSurvivingSpecs`, which the spawn shares; the ledger's `routeSlot`, `credentialSlotForRoute`, `planProviderFor`.
- **Plan** — `model.CredentialPlan` (#2288): the publisher's frozen `(node, provider) → fingerprint` map, joined by the executor's `provider` word; the pool's fine table books donors from it. It never sees the pod.

Each #2022 review round (fleet t49, t50) found a new disagreement:

- **meter ≠ dispatch** — a stamped sandboxed claw walk metered nothing while the container spent the pod's key (t49 r1); under `ITERION_FORBID_SUBSCRIPTION_OAUTH` the pre-flight parks a run on the pod's account for a node `GuardSubscriptionOAuth` refuses (t50 r2, **open**).
- **crossing ≠ in-process**, **meter ≠ meter** — a z.ai key beside a forfait refused a node the forfait funds (t49 r2); the crossing judged the forfait on the raw `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL`, and the route and supervisor meters disagreed behind it (t50 r1).
- **admission ≠ dispatch** — the probe splits the hint on its first comma, the dispatch parses the chain (t50 r2, **open**).
- **backend ≠ backend** — pi and codex read no stamp (t49 r2, deferred).
- **plan ≠ dispatch** (this ADR's review, t50 r1's row on main) — a pool-served claw `anthropic/claude-*` node, the donor's forfait, a gateway `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL` beside the pod's `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`: the call spends the pod key, the plan books the donor. #2288 fixed the class inside its own three derivations (`258bc2fbf`): a fifth answer.

Each fix missed a reader the next round found: the Nth-variant test failing (philosophy 2). Hint-blind auxiliaries ask too (`anthropicFunding`, `ctxFundsProvider`, `forfait.Check`, ADR-131 §7's list). The stamp (`RunBundle.AmbientLLMRefused`) has no producer before #2005 PR-C': what it binds is free to decide today.

## Decision

1. **One resolver.** `pkg/backend/credroute`: `Resolve(ctx, Call) (Spend, error)` over a registry of per-backend **arms**. An arm is its backend's precedence moved out of the dispatch verbatim, registered from its own package; a backend with no arm answers `no-arm` (7), never an engine `if`.
2. **Inputs.** `Call`: backend, spec, ONE leg's hint, role (node, supervisor, auxiliary), sandbox (`none` — the host, a degraded `auto` included — or the driver's host-state mount, `SandboxHomeState`). **Legs**: `credroute.Legs` over `model.RouteView` (workflow, overrides, vars, allowlist) is `resolveProviderChain` exported — override collapse, expansion, `dropForbidden`; the executor, the pre-flight (`RouteViewForDispatch`) and admission (through the executor seam that hands it `EffectiveBackend`) read it, and the other chain parsers go. **Run**: `secrets.Credentials` and the frozen stamps — the allowlist (judging a pod-detected pick too, as `allowlistedDetectorPick` does), the pool stamp, the content boundary. **Posture**: the whole pod environment — every wire, switch and hatch, no name list to drift — each name with its `envtrust` provenance, captured once per attempt through the #2808 seam (`envGetenv`). A planted value funds in process (the local lane's project `.env`), never across the sandbox (D20, ADR-132).
3. **Output.** `Spend{Channel, Slot, Fingerprint, BaseURL, Source}` — Channel: run key, pinned key, run forfait, gateway pair, pod env, host login, own config; Fingerprint: the slot's audit fingerprint (`Credentials.Fingerprint`, what `CredentialPlan` predicts); Source: claude_code's session label (`slotOfSource`'s grammar) — or a refusal (7). The dispatch builds the client and the spawn env from it, never walking again. The crossing hands the container the Spend and what today's per-name classes forward (routing names, header identity lines, the hatch, the forbid switch; planted and stamp-withheld names as explicit empties). Inside, the same `Resolve` runs over what crossed: the node's call lands on the handed Spend or refuses; a subagent's spec (the `agent` tool) resolves by the same arm. The meter key is the meter's scope (tenant, org, pinned) × the Spend's slot and fingerprint.
4. **Readers.** *Dispatch* builds from the Spend and stamps it on its usage events (8). *Admission* runs at ONE site every start passes — after the sandbox resolution (mode, driver, host-state mount), before its host exits (`none`, a degraded `auto`) and any pull, mount or start — on fork-lane runs and every stamped run. It resolves every leg of every node: a node with none resolving is refused, all such nodes named, sorted; a refused `fallbacks:` entry or supervisor is warned. *Meter*: the pre-flight walks the same legs and keys each node's first resolving leg, the one dispatch spends first; a node with none is not metered (refused at launch or dispatch); whether a capped leg's fall-through escapes the park stays `ladderEscapes`' question; `forSource("")` stays for a route the walk cannot read. *Supervisors*: the spawn and the pre-flight keep the specs `Resolve` funds, dropping the rest with today's named warning: `PoolSurvivingSpecs` becomes the `content-boundary` refusal, and `AmbientSurvivingSpecs`' prefix rule goes (a stamped run's sealed vendor key serves its supervisor; ADR-131 §7 refuses the environment only). The publisher's walk and `CredentialPlan` keep predicting what to seal, sharing only the leg grammar (`ir.SplitProviderStep`).
5. **(a) What the stamp binds — operator.** (i) *Recommended*: every backend, as the bundle field reads ("funded by the credentials of this bundle ONLY"). pi and codex refuse the pod's money. kimi, grok and opencode read no run credential, so their stamped legs are refused unconditionally: a node made only of them refuses the launch, a `fallbacks:` rung is warned and never serves (feature-dev's `kimi_quota`; copilot's kimi and grok, `sandbox: none` and judged all the same). (ii) claw and claude_code only (ADR-131 §7's letter, the bench): the rest spend and meter the pod. (iii) (i) for pi and codex; own-config backends admitted with a named warning, metered on the pod. No run is stamped before PR-C'.
6. **(b) pi's pod money under the stamp — operator.** (i) *Recommended*: all the run did not seal — `piAnthropicEnvChannels`†, every name `piCredentialEnvNames` forwards, pi's store (`~/.pi/agent/auth.json`, `ITERION_PI_AGENT_DIR`, `PI_CODING_AGENT_DIR`), the openai-codex bridge's host fallback (`piLoadCodexView` → `CODEX_HOME` or `~/.codex/auth.json`). The run's sealed money serves: its bound keys, its ChatGPT forfait through the bridge (`piCodexSeed` seeds `OAuthDir("codex")` into a per-attempt agent dir). Any other spawn gets explicit empties and an empty agent dir; the bridge's "leave pi to its own login" exits refuse. (ii) The same, but an operator-pinned agent dir may serve. (iii) The Anthropic channels only; thirty-odd provider keys stay spendable.
7. **(c) The refusal — proposed.** Scope: one leg of one call; chain semantics unchanged. Type: (i) today's per-backend types, each reader learning all; (ii) *recommended*: one `*credroute.Refusal{Backend, Provider, Spec, Leg, Reason}` unwrapping to each site's error today (claude_code's stamp `*ErrNoFacadeCredential`, the allowlist's `ErrProviderOutsideAllowlist` / `ErrRouteUnreadableUnderAllowlist`, the boundary's `ErrPoolContentRefused`), so every `errors.As` keeps matching; (iii) one type, no unwrap, every reader edited. Reason: ambient-stamp, subscription-switch, hint-unfunded, no-credential, outside-allowlist, content-boundary, no-arm. Admission keeps `SANDBOX_CREDENTIAL_UNREACHABLE` for no-credential; stamp and switch get their own codes and remedies. The meter reads a refusal as not metered. `GuardSubscriptionOAuth` becomes an arm refusal, closing t50 r2's open MEDIUM.
8. **(d) What the ledger and the fine table learn — operator** (amends #2288). The `fingerprint` key on delegate events, their `folded_spend` slices and `model_served_via_facade` is the session LABEL (claude_code's source, claw's `claw:<provider>`), which resume and `NodesServed` key on and the ledger reads as a source (`routeSlot`, `planProviderFor`); its meaning stays. (i) claw's events carry the hint, the ledger re-derives: one more derivation. (ii) Main's join, `provider` word to plan row: plan ≠ dispatch stays. (iii) *Recommended*: every usage event (delegate events, their slices, `llm_step_finished`) gains the Spend's `credential_channel`, `credential_slot` and `credential_fingerprint`, written by the process that built the call; the ledger books on the slot, the fine table on the fingerprint, the label leaves the ledger. The plan stays a prediction: a disagreeing observation books on its own fingerprint and warns. An event without the keys is a miss: booked on nobody, the fine table voided to the coarse booking (#2288's rule).
9. **Migration — from main, dispatch first, claw first.**
   - *U0*: rebase the bench onto main (#2808 and #2288 rewrote `registry.go`, `claw_backend.go` and `loop_cred_spend.go` since; each conflict becomes a parity row). Each row (posture × run credentials and stamps × backend × spec × leg × role × sandbox × plan) asserts that the built client or crossed env, the admission verdict, the meter key and the booked fingerprint agree. Seen red: the two open t50 r2 rows, the plan row, and this ADR's review rows (a fall-through leg unmetered, an allowlist-dropped leg admitted, an unsandboxed stamped start unjudged).
   - *U1, claw*: the registry walk, the crossing and the switch guard move into the arm; `credroute.Legs` replaces the chain parse; `ClawSandboxCredential` becomes `Resolve`, and admission moves to its one site; the bench's witnesses keep their assertions.
   - *U2, the meter*: the pre-flight on `credroute.Legs` and the Spend (`AnthropicWireRoutes`' chain read goes), the supervisor filters on `Resolve`, the credential keys on claw's events, the fine table on the fingerprint. U0–U2 land as one PR (no red row lands); #2022 closes. ADR-155's units 4 and 6 rebase on it; its other units start from main.
   - *U3* claude_code; *U4* pi and codex under 5 and 6, whose rows alone change expected values; `credentialSlotForRoute`, `planProviderFor` and `routeSlot`'s label read go. *U5* the auxiliaries.
   - *U6*: the #2808 seam's source scan widens to `pkg/backend/delegate`, `pkg/supervise` and the meter, and its process-scoped sites (the registry factories, `OpenAIForfaitServes`) move behind the arms: no env read on the LLM path bypasses the posture.
   - **Ordering with #2005 — operator.** Recommended: PR-C' after U4, so the first stamped run meets one answer on every backend.

## Consequences

- A new posture, stamp or credential kind is one arm edit plus its parity rows; a new chain form, one `credroute.Legs` edit.
- The meter and the fine table book only what the call spent, on the leg dispatch spends. Admission cannot admit a node the dispatch refuses on the runs it judges, sandboxed or not; a trusted unstamped run's refusals still arrive at dispatch.
- The container's node call is checked against the host's Spend. Behaviour changes only where a parity row says so — a stamped run's supervisor on its own sealed key is one.
- Usage events gain three `credential_*` keys, additively; the `fingerprint` label keeps its readers outside the ledger; the re-derivations are deleted, not kept as a fallback.
- The bench does not land as it stands: rebased in U0, its witnesses are the parity spec.
- Out of scope: the publisher's walk, the plan's derivation, the pool's lending.
